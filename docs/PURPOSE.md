# Purpose

## Why
Chinese-speaking applicants and new students cannot easily picture the campus from a flat illustrated map. The idea came from Homescape (a 3D dollhouse dashboard for Home Assistant): borrow the "click a place in a 3D model to learn about it" interaction, not the smart-home part. Not for decorating or planning rooms; it is a campus **preview**.

## Audience
Prospective students, newly admitted international students (mainly Chinese-speaking), and parents. Secondary: staff who answer questions about locations.

## Goals
1. Recognise each building, its majors, offices and facilities.
2. Rehearse the first-day route from Daedong Station to the check-in buildings.
3. Know which gate and which bus to use.
4. Be embeddable in the Chinese-language website without a backend.

## Non-goals
Not an official map or navigation service; no accounts, no data collection; no real-time bus or room availability.
